\begin{abstract}
Omron PLCs dominate automation at PT XYZ and most of them use the FINS protocol. However, almost all open-source HMI platforms—including the lightweight, web-based FUXA—do not natively support FINS. This study develops and integrates a dedicated FINS communication module into FUXA.

An iterative approach was used: literature review, FINS protocol analysis, module implementation, and testing on an Omron CJ2M PLC. Testing shows that the module reliably reads and writes data to the PLC with minimal delay, and FUXA screens now accurately represent real factory-floor processes. The result is a free, fully customizable, browser-based HMI capable of native communication with Omron PLCs without gateways or middleware.

A key limitation identified is an alarm detection delay of approximately 1000~ms, caused by FUXA’s minimum alarm polling interval of 1 second. This limitation was not addressed because the focus of this work is enabling FINS communication rather than redesigning FUXA’s alarm engine.

This development expands the viability of modern open-source tools in Omron-dominated industrial environments and accelerates digital-transformation initiatives in manufacturing.
\end{abstract}
